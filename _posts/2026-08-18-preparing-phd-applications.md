Almost a year into my M.Sc. research, I'm now preparing to apply for PhD programmes that continue my work on impact-resistant, energy-absorbing architected materials — non-linear solid and structural mechanics, crashworthiness of lattice and composite structures, and additive manufacturing.

Since starting at Sakarya University, I've been developing finite-element models in LS-DYNA to predict deformation, force–displacement response, and absorbed energy in 3D-printed chain-fabric structures, designing new convex and concave lattice topologies, and validating numerical predictions against experimental impact data from FDM-fabricated specimens. A review manuscript on functionally graded polymer lattice structures enabled by multi-material fused deposition modeling is currently under review at *MDPI Polymers*, and a manuscript on Doum Palm shell–recycled LDPE composites from my undergraduate research has been submitted for publication.

I'm also looking forward to an Erasmus+ exchange at VŠB – Technical University of Ostrava in Spring 2027, which I expect will broaden the experimental and numerical toolset I bring into a PhD.

This site will be where I post updates as the application process — and the research — moves forward.
