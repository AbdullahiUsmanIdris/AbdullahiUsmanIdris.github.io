This month I began my M.Sc. in Mechanical Design and Manufacturing at Sakarya University, joining the MMY Structural Mechanics & Additive Manufacturing Research Lab under the supervision of Asst. Prof. Dr. Muhammet Muaz Yalçın.

My thesis will investigate the impact resistance of vacuum-formed and PU resin foam-reinforced 3D-printed chain-fabric structures — combining explicit-dynamics finite element modelling in LS-DYNA with FDM specimen fabrication and physical impact testing. It's a natural continuation of the work I started as an undergraduate at Abubakar Tafawa Balewa University (ATBU), where my thesis looked at natural-fiber composites for crash helmet applications, and of my time as a CAD & Simulation Analyst running FEA studies at Etona Engineering Services.

Getting here also meant a year of intensive Turkish-language preparation at Sakarya University's TÖMER programme, where I reached CEFR C1. I'm looking forward to sharing progress on the research here as it develops.
