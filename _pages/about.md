I am an M.Sc. researcher in Mechanical Design and Manufacturing at [Sakarya University](https://www.sakarya.edu.tr/en), Türkiye, where I work in the MMY Structural Mechanics & Additive Manufacturing Research Lab under the supervision of Asst. Prof. Dr. Muhammet Muaz Yalçın. My research interests sit at the intersection of non-linear solid and structural mechanics, crashworthiness, and additive manufacturing: I study how lattice and composite structures absorb energy under compressive and impact loading, and how architected materials produced by additive manufacturing can be designed to improve that performance.

My current thesis investigates the impact resistance of vacuum-formed and PU resin foam-reinforced 3D-printed chain-fabric structures. This work combines explicit-dynamics finite element modelling in LS-DYNA with FDM specimen fabrication and physical impact testing, using the experimental results to validate the numerical predictions. Alongside this, I have designed convex and concave lattice topologies as an extension of conventional lattice architectures for energy-absorption applications, and I am completing a review manuscript on functionally graded polymer lattice structures enabled by multi-material fused deposition modeling.

Before coming to Türkiye, I completed my B.Eng. in Mechanical Engineering at Abubakar Tafawa Balewa University (ATBU), Bauchi, Nigeria, graduating with First Class Honors (Top 1% of my class). My undergraduate thesis developed and characterized a Doum Palm Shell–recycled LDPE composite for crash helmet applications, work that grew out of a nine-month experimental programme on sustainable, natural-fiber-reinforced polymer composites.

I am an incoming Erasmus+ exchange student at VŠB – Technical University of Ostrava, Czech Republic, for Spring 2027, and I am currently preparing to apply for PhD programmes that continue this line of work on impact-resistant, energy-absorbing architected materials.

Research Interests
======
* Non-linear solid and structural mechanics
* Finite element modelling under compressive or impact loading conditions
* Crashworthiness and energy-absorbing potential of lattice and composite structures
* Additive manufacturing of architected materials
* Experimental–numerical correlations and mechanical characterization
