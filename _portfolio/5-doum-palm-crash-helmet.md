**Institution:** Abubakar Tafawa Balewa University (ATBU), Bauchi, Nigeria &nbsp;|&nbsp; **Nov 2022 – Aug 2023**

My undergraduate thesis, *Development and Characterization of Doum Palm Shell–Recycled LDPE Composite for Crash Helmet Applications*, grew out of a nine-month experimental research programme on sustainable composite materials.

* Led development of natural fiber-recycled polymer composites, testing 5 formulations
* Achieved a peak impact strength of 66.67 kJ/m², evaluating suitability for crash helmet shells
* Findings developed into a manuscript, *Mechanical Performance of Doum Palm Shell–Recycled LDPE Hybrid Composites for Safety Helmet Applications* (see [Publications](/publications/))
