Design, SolidWorks modelling, finite element analysis, and physical testing of a screw-extruder briquetting machine that converts agricultural and forestry residues (sawdust, with wheat flour as binder) into energy-dense briquettes, evaluated under no-load and load conditions for mechanical efficiency, operator safety, and briquette quality.
