This review synthesizes the current state of functionally graded polymer lattice structures produced by multi-material fused deposition modeling (FDM), covering design strategies for spatially varying lattice topology, density, and material composition; process considerations for multi-material FDM; and the resulting effects on mechanical performance, including energy absorption and crashworthiness. The manuscript identifies open challenges in process–structure–property relationships for these architected materials and outlines future perspectives for the field.
