This work develops and mechanically characterizes hybrid composites combining Doum Palm shell particulate with recycled low-density polyethylene (LDPE) as a sustainable, natural-fiber-reinforced alternative for crash helmet shells. Five formulations were tested, achieving a peak impact strength of 66.67 kJ/m², and mechanical performance is evaluated for suitability in safety helmet applications. The study grew out of a nine-month undergraduate research programme on sustainable composite materials at Abubakar Tafawa Balewa University (ATBU).
